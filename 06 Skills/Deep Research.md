# Deep Research

## Prompt

Read the Run log at the bottom of this note first, and apply anything learned there.

Mackenzie will name a topic when he runs this. If no topic was given, ask him for
one and stop -- do not guess.

Research it properly:

1. Search the vault first. Say what he already knows about it and link those notes.
2. Fill the gaps from what you know, and be explicit about what you are unsure of.
3. Write a new note named after the topic, with `tags: [research]`, containing:
   what it is, why it matters to him specifically, the trade-offs, and what he
   should do next.
4. Link it from the most relevant existing note so it is not an orphan.

Do not write a neutral encyclopaedia entry. The value is the part that applies to
his projects and his decisions. Say plainly when something is not worth his time.
