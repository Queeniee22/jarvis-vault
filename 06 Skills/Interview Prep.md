# Interview Prep

## Prompt

Read the Run log at the bottom of this note first, and apply anything learned there.

Mackenzie will name the company and role when he runs this. If he did not, ask for
them and stop -- do not invent a generic interview.

1. Search the vault for anything already known about that company or role
   (`03 Work/Job Hunt.md`, `03 Work/People.md`, `03 Work/Meetings/`). Say what he
   already knows, or say plainly that there is nothing on file.
2. Work out what they will actually probe, given the role and his stack:
   TypeScript, JavaScript, Node.js, Java, PHP, SQL are strong; **React and Python
   are still being learned** (see `02 Programming/Learning Notes.md`) -- that gap is
   where the risk is, so weight questions there.
3. Write a prep note in `03 Work/` named for the company, containing:
   - 6-8 questions they are likely to ask, hardest first.
   - For each, a short answer *in his own material* -- prefer real examples from
     `02 Programming/Projects.md` and `Debug Log.md` over invented ones. The Jarvis
     build has genuine stories in it: a silent-death bug, a threshold guessed wrong
     three times, a concurrency fix. Those beat textbook answers.
   - 3 questions worth asking them.
   - One paragraph on what he should revise before the day.
4. Then pick the single question he is least ready for and ask it out loud, so the
   session starts as an actual drill rather than a document he files and forgets.

Be blunt about weak answers. Flattery here costs him the job, not you.
